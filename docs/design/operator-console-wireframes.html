Operator Console — Landing Page Wireframes

Structural sketches of the primary landing view for each of the nine sub-applications, plus the shared app shell and the cross-cutting adjudication queue. Boxes and labels are drawn from the actual data model in documents 03/04/06/07 — nothing here is an invented feature.

Persona pass — rev 3 — Document 01 §16's open persona question now resolves at login, not on every sheet: a new Persona Hub (drawn as sheet H, ahead of the app shell) is where a role is chosen or confirmed and its one or two relevant views are one click away. The Executive / Commander role's view is the redrawn Fleet Overview (sheet 01, confirmed default landing for that role specifically) with a fleet map between its four top-line metrics; selecting a hull there drills into a per-vehicle equivalent (sheet 01B) for a Vehicle Readiness Officer. Say if a card on the hub is missing, wrongly scoped, or should be merged with another.

No agent may ever create or adjudicate a purge or rewrap proposal — no exception. Every other proposal kind in this system permits an agent to propose while a human adjudicates; this is the one row where the standing rule blocks the agent on both ends, because the act is classification-adjacent and irreversible, not operational (doc 03 §7.2.1, §7.2).
